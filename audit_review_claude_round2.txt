### 2. Spending-change savings are phantom — `forecast_engine.py:265`
`evaluate_spending_changes` takes the savings of a **single historical transaction** (`amount − minimum_allowed_amount`) and compares it to the 90-day shortfall. It never re-simulates the timeline, and `is_schedule_safe` is never called on this candidate.

All three affected rows draw their savings from **`dining`**, and `dining` is never modeled in the forecast at all — `prot_cats` at `forecast_engine.py:140` is `protect ∪ {groceries, transport} − FIXED_COMMITMENT_CATEGORIES`, and none of these users protect dining:

Reducing a *past, settled, one-off* dining charge in a category the projection ignores moves the projected balance by exactly **zero**. All three rows recommend paying the full amount today when `amount_safe_to_pay < requested_amount`, which breaches `minimum_balance_to_keep` by the shortfall.
